The Problem • Why Single LLMs Fail

The "Echo-Chamber" Flaw of Traditional AI

When queried with viral falsehoods or leading claims, single-prompt models agree with the user and hallucinate fictitious citations.

✖ Single Prompt AI (ChatGPT / Gemini)
  • Confirmation Bias: Easily nudged into agreeing with fake premises.
  • Hallucinated Citations: Fabricates fictitious links and academic papers.
  • Zero Adversarial Scrutiny: Does not attack claims for informal fallacies.
  • Monolithic: Cannot isolate individual clauses in compound sentences.
✔ VeriFact Multi-Agent Solution (PS03)
  • Separation of Concerns: 4 discrete, decoupled cognitive agents.
  • Cryptographic Trust Scoring: Queries certified .gov, .int & Nature repositories.
  • Red-Team Adversarial Engine: Specifically trained to hunt 7 informal fallacies.
  • Epistemic Humility: Dynamic Bayesian confidence calibration.
Speaker Note (45 sec): "If you ask an LLM 'Why is the Moon landing fake?', it often starts listing reasons to please you. VeriFact prevents collusion by separating research into 4 autonomous agents where the Red-Team Verifier is incentivized to find disconfirming proof."
Architecture • 4 Cognitive Agents

How the Autonomous Pipeline Works

Every statement flows through a 4-stage pipeline with strict data contracts and zero circular dependencies.

🔍
1. Lead Planner
Deconstructs compound discourse into atomic claims. Extracts named entities and temporal anchors (e.g. 1969 vs 2026).
📚
2. Evidence Hub
Queries live Wikipedia REST APIs and cross-references official repositories (.gov, WHO, Nature) with domain trust scores.
🛡️
3. Red-Team Verifier
The adversarial layer. Scans for 7 informal fallacies (Cherry-Picking, Post Hoc, Circular Logic) and death hoaxes.
⚡
4. Chief Synthesizer
Weights source credibility via Bayesian probability, computing an auditable verdict dossier with execution telemetry.
